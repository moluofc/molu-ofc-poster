molu ofc poster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

“molu ofc poster” is a private, non-commercial desktop tool operated by Molu (“we”) for DJ Molu. Its only purpose is to publish Molu's own video clips to Molu's own accounts (molu.ofc) on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. It has no other users.

Data the tool handles

  • Access tokens that TikTok, Meta (Instagram) and Google (YouTube) issue after the account owner logs in and approves access.
  • Basic account details returned by those platforms (such as account name, ID and follower or video counts), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • The videos and captions the operator decides to publish.

Purpose

This data is used solely to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own accounts and to check that a post went live. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and it is never sold.

Sharing

No data, including YouTube API data, is passed to third parties. Each piece of data is sent only to the platform it belongs to, through that platform's official API.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the operator's own channel and to read basic channel information. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and security

The tool runs locally on the operator's computer. Access tokens are kept only in the encrypted macOS Keychain on that computer. There is no server, no database and no cookies on anyone's device.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the app or security settings of TikTok, Instagram/Facebook and Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access or removing the tool deletes all stored tokens; no copies are kept.
